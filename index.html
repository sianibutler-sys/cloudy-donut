          Our story

          Built in Baltimore. Owned by us.

          Derrick Faulcon founded Cloudy Donut in Baltimore in 2019 and opened the first shop in 2020. He named it for the way the donuts eat: light and airy, like biting into a cloud.

          Every donut is made from scratch, in house, in small batches. Applesauce, coconut milk and shortening stand in for eggs and dairy, so the whole menu is 100% vegan.

          Derrick and his partner in life and business, Zewiditu Jewel, took Cloudy to New York and made history twice: the first Black-owned food and beverage business in Brooklyn Heights, then again in Nolita.

          Cloudy Donut is one stream of 7Streams, Derrick's brand.

          Good to know

          Are your donuts gluten free?

          Our donuts are 100% vegan, but they are not gluten free.

          Which donuts have nuts?

          Every flavor that contains nuts is marked in the flavor list above.

          What do you use in place of eggs and dairy?

          Applesauce, coconut milk and shortening. All donuts are fried in liquid vegetable shortening.

          How long do the donuts keep?

          There are no preservatives, so we recommend eating donuts within 13 hours at room temperature. Cinnamon rolls and pound cakes keep for 2–3 days.

          Do you ship?

          Not donuts. They're too delicate to travel. Pound cakes are coming next year, and the waitlist hears first.

          Do you make custom donuts?

          We offer holiday and occasion packages, but not custom donuts at this time.

          Do you sell gift cards?

          Gift cards are available in store at our Brooklyn Heights shop.

          Something for the dog?

          Yes. Lil Bow Wow and the Snoop Doggy Donut are made for pups.

          What this build does

          Yours to run, yours to keep

          1. The lineup updates itself

            Staff change this week's flavors, shop hours and pop-up dates in one Airtable base. The site reads it live, so nobody opens a site editor.

          2. Open now, at a glance

            Each shop shows whether it's open right now, in New York time.

          3. A home for the tour

            Every stop, date and sold-out flag in one place, plus a "come to my city" list that shows where demand is.

          4. Cake shipping, ready before launch

            A waitlist that builds now, so the first day of shipping already has buyers.

          5. Every form lands in one place

            Catering, newsletter, tour alerts and the waitlist go to Airtable and to email.

          6. Owned by Cloudy

            Custom code in your own GitHub and Cloudflare accounts. No platform subscription.
