      What changes for people using screen readers, keyboards or a phone, on every page.

      1. Today

        Mint text on cream measures about 1.5:1. WCAG AA asks for 4.5:1.

        New

        Text contrast meets WCAG AA on every page.

      2. Today

        Photos are described to screen readers by file name.

        New

        Photos carry real descriptions, or stay silent when the donut's name sits beside them.

      3. Today

        Flavors and Locations have no H1. Contact and FAQ jump from H1 to H3.

        New

        One H1 per page, no skipped heading levels.

      4. Today

        Instagram and Facebook open new tabs without saying so.

        New

        Links that open a new tab tell screen readers first.

      5. Today

        No phone number on the site can be tapped to call.

        New

        Every phone number calls on tap.

      6. Today

        The email address appears only on Contact.

        New

        Email sits in the footer of every page.

      7. Today

        Log In and a cart sit in every header. Nothing is sold online.

        New

        No login or cart until there's something to sell.

      8. Today

        Six leftover Wix template posts, on fintech and earbuds, are indexed by Google under the Cloudy name.

        New

        Only Cloudy pages exist to be indexed.

      Automated scan with axe-core against WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard used in ADA website claims: 0 issues on all six pages, with search, the flavor cards and this panel open.

      Yours to run, yours to keep

      1. The lineup updates itself

        Flavors, shop hours and pop-up dates live in one Airtable base. Change it there and every page follows.

      2. Every pop-up gets a flyer

        Add a city and date, and the site builds the "Cloudy Donut Presents" flyer with a live countdown.

      3. Demand, mapped

        "Bring Cloudy to my city" signups show where the next tour stop should go.

      4. Shipping, ready before launch

        The cake waitlist builds now, so the first day of shipping already has buyers.

      5. One inbox for every form

        Experiences, newsletter, tour alerts and the waitlist land in Airtable and email.

      6. Owned by Cloudy

        Custom code in Cloudy's own GitHub and Cloudflare accounts. No platform subscription.
