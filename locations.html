    Good To Know

    Where are you all located?

    We have two shops in NYC: 14 Columbia Pl., Brooklyn, NY 11201 and 209 Mott Street, New York, NY 10012. Our Lauraville home in Baltimore hosts pop-ups. See hours and maps.

    What oils are your donuts made in?

    All our donuts are fried in liquid vegetable shortening.

    What substitutes do you use to make the donuts vegan?

    Applesauce, coconut milk and shortening are used in place of animal products.

    Are your donuts gluten free?

    Our donuts are 100% vegan, but they are not gluten free.

    Which donuts have nuts in them?

    Some of our donuts contain nuts. Every flavor with nuts is marked on the flavors page, and you can filter to nut-free flavors.

    How long can I store the donuts for?

    Our donuts do not contain preservatives, so we do not recommend holding them longer than 13 hours at room temperature. Our cinnamon rolls and pound cakes have a shelf life of 2–3 days.

    What happens if I place a preorder and forget to pick it up?

    Contact the company where you placed your order, like DoorDash or Grubhub, to sort it out. If you ordered directly from us, give us a call at 347.463.9628 and we will do our best to accommodate you.

    Do you cater or do large orders?

    Yes. Tell us about your event on the Experiences page, or call 347.463.9628 to place a large order.

    Do you all do custom or special order donuts?

    We have special holiday and occasion packages, but we do not make custom order donuts at this time.

    Do you all ship?

    Due to the short shelf life of our vegan donuts, we do not ship them. Pound cakes are coming next year, and the waitlist hears first.

    Do you all wholesale the donuts?

    We do not wholesale our donuts at this time, but call 347.463.9628 to learn how we can help with your program or event.

    Do you sell gift cards?

    Gift cards are available in store at our Brooklyn Heights location.

    Do you all sell merchandise?

    Call 347.463.9628 or DM @cloudydonuts to ask what merch is in stock.

    How can I apply for a job?

    Email us at info@cloudydonut.com. We are always open to new talent with an interest in baking.

    Something for the dog?

    Yes. Lil Bow Wow and the Snoop Doggy Donut are made for pups.
