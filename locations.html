    Good To Know

    Are your donuts gluten free?

    Our donuts are 100% vegan, but they are not gluten free.

    Which donuts have nuts?

    Every flavor with nuts is marked on the flavors page.

    What replaces eggs and dairy?

    Applesauce, coconut milk and shortening. All donuts are fried in liquid vegetable shortening.

    How long do they keep?

    No preservatives, so eat donuts within 13 hours at room temperature. Cinnamon rolls and pound cakes keep for 2–3 days.

    What if I forget to pick up a preorder?

    If you ordered through a delivery app, contact that app. If you ordered directly from us, give us a call and we'll do our best to help.

    Do you ship?

    Not donuts. Pound cakes are coming next year, and the waitlist hears first.

    Do you make custom donuts?

    We have special holiday and occasion packages, but no custom orders at this time.

    Something for the dog?

    Lil Bow Wow and the Snoop Doggy Donut are made for pups.

    Gift cards?

    Available in store at Brooklyn Heights.

    How can I apply for a job?

    Send us an email. We're always open to new talent with an interest in baking.

    What this build does

    Yours to run, yours to keep

    1. The lineup updates itself

      Flavors, shop hours and pop-up dates live in one Airtable base. Change it there and every page follows.

    2. Every pop-up gets a flyer

      Add a city and date, and the site builds the "Cloudy Donut Presents" flyer with a live countdown.

    3. Demand, mapped

      "Bring Cloudy to my city" signups show where the next tour stop should go.

    4. Shipping, ready before launch

      The cake waitlist builds now, so the first day of shipping already has buyers.

    5. One inbox for every form

      Experiences, newsletter, tour alerts and the waitlist land in Airtable and email.

    6. Owned by Cloudy

      Custom code in Cloudy's own GitHub and Cloudflare accounts. No platform subscription.
